## 三、长描述（Long Description）

### 3.1 英文长描述

```
About This Game

Battleplan is a pixel fantasy real-time strategy game where you command elemental soul warriors in fast-paced arena battles.

🎮 Core Gameplay
- Command your soul army in real-time tactical battles
- Select, move, and attack with intuitive RTS controls
- Cast powerful elemental skills: Fireball, Water Jet, Rock Spike, Wind Blade, Heal, and Shield
- Manage energy (MP) and cooldowns strategically

🔥 Four Elemental Souls
- Fire (IGNIS): High attack power, explosive damage
- Water (AQUA): Balanced stats, healing and support
- Earth (TERRA): High defense, tanky and durable
- Wind (VENTUS): Fast speed, rapid attacks and evasion

📈 Soul Growth System
- Level up your souls through battle experience
- Unlock and upgrade powerful skills
- Enhance attributes: ATK, DEF, SPD, HP, MP
- Evolve your soul to unlock new forms and abilities

🏠 Soul Home
- Relax and interact with your soul companions
- Train your souls to improve their stats
- Feed, play, and talk with your souls to increase intimacy
- Customize your soul home with decorations

🎨 Pixel Fantasy Art Style
- Beautiful 2D pixel art with deep purple and gold color palette
- Detailed pixel sprite animations for all soul units
- Magical particle effects for skills and combat
- Atmospheric pixel art backgrounds for each scene

🎯 Battle System
- Real-time combat with strategic depth
- Critical hits with special effects and screen shake
- Damage numbers floating above units
- Health and energy orbs for clear visualization
- Battle results with detailed statistics and rewards

🏆 Achievements
- 24 achievements across combat, growth, collection, and challenge categories
- Unlock rare titles and soul skins
- Test your skills in high-difficulty challenges

📝 Early Access Plan
- M2 (Current): Core RTS gameplay, soul selection, battle system, soul home
- M3 (Next Update): More soul units, skill system expansion, shop and item system, tutorial system
- Future: Multiplayer PvP, ranked mode, more arenas, story mode

Join the soul battle arena and become the ultimate soul commander!
```

- 通过战斗经验升级你的灵魂
- 解锁和升级强大技能

- 进化你的灵魂，解锁新形态和能力

加入灵魂对战竞技场，成为终极灵魂指挥官！

## 六、游戏特色（Features）

### 6.1 核心特色列表
1. **快节奏RTS战斗** - 实时战术战斗，简单上手，深度策略
2. **四大元素灵魂** - 火/水/土/风，每种都有独特属性和技能
3. **灵魂成长系统** - 升级、技能解锁、属性提升、进化
4. **灵魂之家互动** - 与灵魂伙伴互动、训练、喂食、玩耍
5. **精致像素艺术** - 深紫+金色调，精致像素奇幻风格
6. **魔法粒子特效** - 技能释放和战斗的华丽粒子特效
7. **24个成就** - 战斗、成长、收集、挑战四大分类
8. **持续更新** - Early Access阶段持续更新内容

### 6.2 特色卖点（Key Selling Points）
- **像素奇幻RTS** - 独特的像素艺术风格+即时战略玩法
- **灵魂收集养成** - 收集、训练、进化你的灵魂战士
- **元素魔法系统** - 四大元素，六种技能，策略组合
- **轻松上手** - 简单的RTS操作，新手也能快速上手
- **深度策略** - 技能冷却、能量管理、单位操作，有策略深度
- **独立游戏** - 小团队精心制作，持续更新
